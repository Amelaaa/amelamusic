Thesis Poster

Poster design for my master's thesis. Combining a Figma prototype of a music streaming platform with a print-ready visual layout.

Role Visual Designer & UX Designer
Context Master's Thesis, UX Design
Tools Figma · Canva · Adobe Photoshop
Year 2026

Project Overview

This poster was created to present my master's thesis at the thesis fair. The mobiles visible in the poster show a prototype of a music streaming platform that I designed in Figma as part of the study.

The poster design was built in Canva to meet the thesis fair's print standards, while the visual style was intentionally designed to match the aesthetic of the Figma prototype — creating a cohesive look across both the research presentation and the design work.

My Contributions

Prototype Design

  • Designed a music streaming platform prototype in Figma for use in the thesis study.
  • Created a consistent UI with custom typography, color palette, and component design.

Poster Layout & Typography

  • Designed the full poster layout including structure, hierarchy, and content flow.
  • Selected typography and spacing to ensure readability at print scale.

Visual Style & Color

  • Developed a color palette and visual tone that matched the Figma prototype's design language.
  • Ensured visual consistency between the poster and the prototype screens shown within it.

Print Production

  • Adapted the design to meet the thesis fair's printing standards and format requirements.
  • Prepared the final file in Canva for high-quality print output.
