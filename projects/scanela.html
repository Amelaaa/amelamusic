Scanela

A mobile app that turns ingredients into recipe ideas. Scan what's in your fridge, get instant suggestions, and save your favorites.

Role Idea · Design · Development · Marketing
Type Personal Project, Mobile App
Tools Figma · React Native
Year 2026

Project Overview

Scanela is a mobile app I conceived, designed, and built from scratch. You point your camera at the ingredients you have at home, and the app suggests recipes you can make right now.

Beyond scanning, users can import recipes from external sources and save them to a personal collection. I was responsible for every part of the project: the initial idea, UX research and design, development, visual branding, and marketing.

My Contributions

Idea & Concept

  • Identified the problem
  • Defined the core concept
  • Mapped out the full product vision, feature set, and user flow from scratch.

UX Design

  • Conducted user research to understand cooking habits and pain points.
  • Created user personas, user journeys, and information architecture.
  • Designed wireframes and interactive prototypes in Figma.
  • Iterated on the design based on usability testing and feedback.

Visual Design & Branding

  • Developed the full visual identity: logotype, color palette, and typography.
  • Designed all UI components and screens with a consistent design system.
  • Created app icon, splash screen, and marketing visuals.

Development

  • Built the app from the ground up
  • Implemented the camera-based ingredient scanning feature.
  • Built the recipe suggestion logic and import/save functionality.
  • Ensured a smooth, responsive experience across devices.

Marketing

  • Developed the brand voice and positioning for Scanela.
  • Created marketing materials and social content to present the app.
