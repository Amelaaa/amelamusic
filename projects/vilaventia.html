Visual Design · Social Media Branding Sep 2023 — March 2025

Vilaventia

Small business branding and social media design for an independent jewelry business. Focused on visual identity, Instagram content, promotional materials, and short-form video.

Role Visual Designer & Social Media Branding
Context Vilaventia, Independent Jewelry Business
Tools Adobe Illustrator · Photoshop · Instagram · CapCut
Year 2023

Project Overview

This project was created for Vilaventia, a small independent jewelry business. The goal was to strengthen the brand's visual identity and online presence through consistent social media content and promotional materials.

I supported the business by designing Instagram posts, creating posters, and helping maintain the brand's visual tone across digital platforms. The focus was on creating clean, engaging visuals that matched the brand's aesthetic and helped attract customers.

My Contributions

Social Media Content Creation

  • Designed Instagram posts and visuals for the brand's online presence.
  • Created consistent content that supported the jewelry brand's aesthetic.

Motion & Short-Form Video

  • Created short-form video content for social media using CapCut.
  • Adapted visuals for engaging digital formats and promotional use.

Promotional Graphic Design

  • Designed posters and promotional materials for the business.
  • Created clean layouts that aligned with the brand's visual direction.

Visual Branding & Content Support

  • Maintained visual consistency across Instagram and other digital touchpoints.
  • Provided ongoing design support to strengthen the brand identity over time.
