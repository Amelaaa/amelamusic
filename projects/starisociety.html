Stari Society

Independent streetwear brand combining contemporary design with Bosnian identity.

Role Co-founder & Brand Designer
Client Stari Society
Tools Illustrator · Figma · Shopify
Year Juli 2026 — Pågående

Project Overview

Stari Society is an independent streetwear brand we started from the ground up, combining modern clothing with Bosnian identity. The idea is to create pieces that let people carry a part of their roots with them, especially those who live or were born outside of Bosnia.

The first collection focuses on Bosnian cities, lakes, landscapes and symbols, presented through a modern and premium streetwear aesthetic.

My Contributions

Brand Identity

  • Developed the visual direction and overall identity for Stari Society.
  • Created the logo and explored typography, layouts and graphic elements for the brand.
  • Defined a streetwear-inspired direction with a clean and premium feel.

Clothing & Print Design

  • Designed the first collection of T-shirt graphics and prints.
  • Created designs inspired by Bosnian cities, lakes, landscapes and the fleur-de-lis.
  • Developed different visual directions while keeping the collection consistent as a brand.

Shopify & E-commerce

  • Built the Stari Society online store using Shopify.
  • Adapted the theme, layout and content to fit the brand's visual identity.
  • Structured the store and product presentation with a focus on a simple shopping experience.

Social Media & Marketing

  • Created content for TikTok and Instagram to build the brand's online presence.
  • Experimented with fashion content, Bosnian imagery, music, quotes and relatable posts.
  • Focused on organic content and introducing the products naturally rather than relying on traditional advertising.
