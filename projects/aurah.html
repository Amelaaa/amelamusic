Graphic Design · Branding March — May 2025

Aurah

A conceptual perfume brand. Visual identity, packaging design, and digital branding developed through research, experimentation, and generative AI tools.

Role Visual Identity & Packaging Designer
Context Visual Lab. Graphic Design & Web Development
Tools Adobe Illustrator · Photoshop · Adobe Firefly
Year 2025

Project Overview

This project was created as part of the course Visual Lab within the Graphic Design and Web Development program. The objective was to design a future-oriented digital branding concept that combines visual identity, packaging, motion, and generative AI tools.

Aurah is a conceptual perfume brand developed through research, visual exploration, and experimentation with modern branding techniques. The project focuses on creating a cohesive and expressive brand identity across digital and physical touchpoints, including packaging, promotional visuals, and mockups.

My Contributions

Concept Development & Visual Research

  • Defined the brand direction through mood boarding and visual research.
  • Developed a brand story and positioning for Aurah as a future-oriented fragrance label.

Brand Identity Design

  • Created the full color palette, typography system, and visual language for the brand.
  • Designed the logotype and supporting graphic elements in Adobe Illustrator.

Packaging Design & Mockups

  • Designed the perfume bottle, outer box, and shopping bags to reflect the brand aesthetic.
  • Produced high-fidelity mockups in Photoshop for presentation.

Digital Brand Visuals & AI Experimentation

  • Created promotional visuals for digital marketing use.
  • Used Adobe Firefly to generate and refine imagery, integrating AI into the design workflow.
